Her story

Sereko Lebenya is an author and storyteller whose writing lives at the intersection of faith, identity, and the quiet, unhurried work of becoming. Her essays and books do not offer formulas. They offer something rarer: honest company on the road.

Born in Lesotho, she has lived across borders and between worlds, and it is in that in-between space that most of her writing was formed. She writes about migration not as geography but as the interior condition — the way a person must sometimes leave themselves behind in order to be remade.

Her first book, You Are Not Invisible, was written for the woman who has felt unseen. Her second, After Forty, is the book she could only write after she had lived through the decade it describes.

She lives in the East Midlands, where she writes, reads, and tends to the stories that will not leave her alone.

Published · 2024

You Are Not
Invisible

For the woman who has ever felt overlooked, forgotten, or unseen.

Sereko Lebenya writes about invisibility not as metaphor but as lived experience — the quiet accumulation of moments in which a woman wonders whether her presence, her story, her struggle, have been noticed by anyone at all.

You Are Not Invisible is a book of honest, carefully written essays that hold a mirror up to the hidden life. It does not offer easy comfort. It offers something better: the company of a writer who has sat in the same silence, and found, eventually, that God was in it.

For women who have crossed oceans, changed shape, raised children, buried grief, or quietly wondered what comes next: this book was written for you.

Forthcoming

After Forty

The essays that refused to stay private.

After Forty began as notes to herself — observations gathered in the years when everything she thought she understood about her life began to quietly revise itself. The decade of forty, she found, is less a destination than a dismantling. It asks questions that younger years do not know how to ask.

This is a book about beginning again: in faith, in identity, in courage. About the particular freedom that arrives when a woman stops performing her life and starts actually living it.

After Forty is Sereko's most personal book yet. It is also, she believes, the most necessary one.

Be first to know when it arrives

Speaking

Words that belong
in a room

Sereko brings to every stage the same voice that marks her writing: quiet, direct, and unexpectedly moving.

What to expect

Sereko does not perform conviction. She speaks from it. Whether addressing a conference of hundreds or a literary salon of thirty, she brings the same quality of attention: honest, prepared, deeply human.

She is available for faith and women's conferences, literary events, retreat weekends, book clubs, and author panels. Each engagement is tailored to its context — there are no off-the-shelf talks.

On speaking

"Sereko speaks the way she writes: with a quality of honesty that makes the room go quiet and stay quiet."
